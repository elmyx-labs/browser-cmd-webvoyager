## Our only change: dates that had passed

Booking and Google Flights cannot search a date in the past. On those two sites, and only there, every explicit date in
a task is moved forward by whole years until the earliest one is no longer in the past, computed as of 2026-10-27
(`harness/runner/dates.py`). No other word of any task was changed, no task was removed, and the judge is
reference-free, so the roll changes what can be run, not how it is scored. Machine-readable: 
[`benchmark/data/browser-cmd/date_updates.json`](benchmark/data/browser-cmd/date_updates.json).
