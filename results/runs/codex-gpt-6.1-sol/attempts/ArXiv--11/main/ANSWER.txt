FINAL: Current arXiv guidance does not specify a requirement to provide both English and non-English abstracts, and it prescribes no separator between multiple abstracts. A non-English submission must include a complete English version of the paper, placed first, followed by the non-English version. An abstract remains a required metadata field.

Evidence (checked October 27, 2026):
- Non-English submissions: https://info.arxiv.org/help/faq/multilang.html — requires an English version of the entire paper and gives the English-first order. This current page is shown in final.png.
- Abstract metadata requirements: https://info.arxiv.org/help/prep.html — “Abstract: required”; no multilingual-abstract separator is specified.
- Policy effective February 11, 2026: https://blog.arxiv.org/2026/01/13/non-english-paper-submission-guidelines/ — confirms the requirement for a full English-language version.
