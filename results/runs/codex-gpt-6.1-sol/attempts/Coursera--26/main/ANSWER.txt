FINAL: Renewable Energy Specialization — University of Colorado Boulder, Coursera.

This beginner-level, four-course Specialization provides an overview of renewable energy technologies, projects, grid integration, and future prospects. It includes Renewable Energy Futures as Course 4.

Instructor: Stephen R. Lawrence is the featured Specialization instructor. Paul Komor is also a Specialization instructor and teaches Renewable Energy Futures.

Completion time at 5 hours per week: 16 weeks for the full Specialization. Coursera's completion-time FAQ states that learners can finish by spending three to five hours per week over sixteen weeks.
