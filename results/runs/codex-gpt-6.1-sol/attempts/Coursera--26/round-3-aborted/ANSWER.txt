FINAL: Renewable Energy Specialization, offered by the University of Colorado Boulder on Coursera, is the match. It provides a comprehensive overview of renewable energy technologies, projects, grid integration and future prospects, is marked Beginner level, and includes Renewable Energy Futures as course 4.

For the whole Specialization: 16 weeks at 5 hours per week. Coursera's FAQ says it can be completed over sixteen weeks with three to five hours of course content per week. The featured instructor is Stephen R. Lawrence; Paul Komor is also listed as an instructor.

If “the course” means Renewable Energy Futures alone: its instructor is Paul Komor. The Specialization's course list gives 13 hours, so 13 ÷ 5 = 2.6 weeks, or about 3 weeks at 5 hours per week.
