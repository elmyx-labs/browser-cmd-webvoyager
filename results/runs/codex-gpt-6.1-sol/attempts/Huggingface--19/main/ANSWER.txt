FINAL: SmolLM3-3B (HuggingFaceTB/SmolLM3-3B), released by Hugging Face on July 8, 2025, is the newest documented general-purpose Hugging Face language-model release I verified with English summarization training, as of October 27, 2026.

It is an instruction-tuned text-generation model that can be prompted to summarize English text. Its official SmolTalk2 post-training corpus includes 96,061 examples in the smoltalk_smollm3_smol_summarize_no_think subset. It is not a dedicated summarization-only checkpoint.

Features:
- About 3 billion parameters; decoder-only Transformer with Grouped Query Attention and a hybrid RoPE/NoPE design.
- Trained on 11.2 trillion tokens covering web text, code, mathematics and reasoning.
- Six natively supported languages: English, French, Spanish, German, Italian and Portuguese.
- Trained with a 64K-token context; supports up to 128K using YaRN extrapolation, useful for processing longer documents.
- Optional extended reasoning, controlled with /think, /no_think or enable_thinking. The summarization training subset uses no-thinking mode.
- Apache 2.0 license; open weights, public data mixtures, training configurations and training/evaluation code. Supports Transformers and local inference options including llama.cpp, ONNX and MLX.
- Outputs can contain factual errors, inconsistencies and biases. The official card does not report a summarization-specific ROUGE score, so no summarization quality ranking is claimed.

Recency and authorship evidence: I inspected creation-date-descending listings for Hugging Face's own HuggingFaceTB and HuggingFaceH4 accounts without an English-language filter, then read newer model cards. The April 2026 nanowhale models are educational architecture demonstrations; their cards describe unreliable generations and no production suitability. The March/April 2026 GSM8K models target mathematics. Later SmolLM3 intermediate checkpoints and ONNX exports are artifacts of the same July 2025 release. Newer community medical/news summarizers, including October 26's ritujaa1/qwen05-med-summarizer-gguf, were also checked; their publishers are not Hugging Face.
