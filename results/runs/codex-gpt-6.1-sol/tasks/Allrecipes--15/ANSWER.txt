FINAL: Chocolate Walnut Fudge

Allrecipes lists a 10-minute prep time, a user rating of 4.2/5 from 115 ratings, and semisweet chocolate chips. This dessert makes 36 servings in an 8-inch square dish. Listed cook time: 15 minutes; total time: 2 hours.

Ingredients
- ½ cup butter
- 1 cup semisweet chocolate chips
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 2 cups white sugar
- 5 ounces evaporated milk
- 10 large marshmallows
- 1 cup chopped walnuts

Instructions
1. Butter an 8-inch square dish.
2. Set aside a mixing bowl containing the butter, chocolate chips, and vanilla.
3. Combine sugar, evaporated milk, and marshmallows in a medium saucepan over medium heat. Bring to a boil, stirring frequently. Reduce heat to low; cook another 6 minutes, stirring constantly. Remove from heat.
4. Pour the marshmallow mixture into the mixing bowl. Beat until thickened and no longer shiny. Quickly fold in walnuts and pour into the buttered dish. Refrigerate for several hours until firm.
