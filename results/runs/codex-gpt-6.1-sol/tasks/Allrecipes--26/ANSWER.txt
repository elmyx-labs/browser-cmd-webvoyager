FINAL: Meatiest Vegetarian Chili from Your Slow Cooker — Allrecipes

This vegetarian tofu-and-black-bean chili provides 21 g protein per serving and has a 4.6/5 rating from 346 ratings, including 232 written reviews. The original recipe serves 8.

Ingredients
• ½ cup olive oil
• 4 onions, chopped
• 2 green bell peppers, seeded and chopped
• 2 red bell peppers, seeded and chopped
• 1 (14-ounce) package firm tofu, drained and cubed
• 4 cloves garlic, minced
• 4 (15.5-ounce) cans black beans, drained
• 2 (15-ounce) cans crushed tomatoes
• 6 tablespoons chili powder
• 2 tablespoons dried oregano
• 2 tablespoons distilled white vinegar
• 1 tablespoon liquid hot pepper sauce, such as Tabasco
• 2 teaspoons ground cumin
• 2 teaspoons salt
• ½ teaspoon ground black pepper

Time: The recipe lists 25 minutes prep, 6 hours 15 minutes cooking, and 6 hours 40 minutes total. Its directions allow 6–8 hours on Low in the slow cooker.

Method: Heat the olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Cook the onions until they begin to soften, then add bell peppers, tofu and garlic. Cook, stirring, until the vegetables are tender and lightly browned, about 10 minutes. Put the beans in a slow cooker, add the tofu-vegetable mixture and tomatoes, and gently stir in the remaining ingredients. Cover and cook on Low for 6–8 hours.
