Time and yield: 20 minutes preparation, 40 minutes cooking, 1 hour total; serves 6.

Ingredients:
- 2 lb skinless, boneless chicken breast halves
- 3 tsp salt, divided; 3 tbsp cooking oil, or as needed
- 1½ cups chopped onion, 1 tbsp minced garlic, 1½ tsp minced fresh ginger
- 1 tbsp curry powder; 1 tsp each ground cumin, turmeric, coriander, and cayenne pepper
- 1 tbsp water plus ½ cup water, used separately
- One 15-oz can crushed tomatoes and 1 cup plain yogurt
- 2 tbsp chopped fresh cilantro, divided
- 1 tsp garam masala and 1 tbsp fresh lemon juice

Cooking instructions:
1. Season chicken with 2 tsp salt. Heat 1–2 tbsp oil in a large skillet over high heat. Brown the chicken in batches, adding oil as needed, then set it aside while still partially cooked.
2. Reduce heat to medium. Cook onion, garlic, and ginger in the remaining oil for 5–8 minutes, until the onion softens. Add curry powder, cumin, turmeric, coriander, cayenne, and 1 tbsp water; stir for about 1 minute.
3. Stir in tomatoes, yogurt, 1 tbsp cilantro, and the remaining 1 tsp salt.
4. Return chicken and its juices to the skillet. Add ½ cup water, bring to a boil, and turn chicken to coat. Sprinkle with garam masala and the remaining cilantro.
5. Cover and simmer for about 20 minutes, until the chicken reaches at least 165°F (74°C). Finish with lemon juice. Serve with basmati rice or warm naan, as suggested by the recipe.
