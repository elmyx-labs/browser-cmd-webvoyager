Time: 15 minutes prep + 30 minutes cooking = 45 minutes prep and cook time. Allow 10 additional minutes for cooling; the listed total time is 55 minutes.

Ingredients (original 1X recipe):
- 2 cups all-purpose flour
- 2 cups granulated white sugar (choose sugar labeled vegan, as the recipe advises)
- ¾ cup good-quality unsweetened cocoa powder, sifted
- 1 teaspoon baking powder
- ¾ teaspoon salt
- 1 cup hot water or coffee
- ½ cup vegetable oil
- 2 teaspoons vanilla extract

Preparation overview: Heat the oven to 350°F (175°C). Grease a 9×13-inch baking dish with baking spray and line its bottom with parchment. Whisk the dry ingredients together, then mix in the water or coffee, oil, and vanilla. Spread the batter in the dish and bake for about 30 minutes, until the top loses its shine and the center feels just set. Cool for at least 10 minutes, then cut into 15 squares.
