Original recipe (1X) yields 15 servings. Ingredients: 2 cups all-purpose flour; 2 cups granulated white sugar; ¾ cup good-quality unsweetened cocoa powder, sifted; 1 teaspoon baking powder; ¾ teaspoon salt; 1 cup hot water or coffee; ½ cup vegetable oil; 2 teaspoons vanilla extract.
Recipe directions: preheat 350 F / 175 C, grease 9x13-inch baking dish and line bottom with parchment, whisk dry ingredients, mix wet into dry, spread batter, bake about 30 min until top no longer shiny and center just set, cool at least 10 minutes and cut into 15 squares.
