Title: Spanish Paella. Submitted by Christine L. Updated September 11, 2026.
Qualifying evidence: “4.7”; “(178)”; “124 Reviews”. Reviews section confirms “4.7 out of 5”, “178 Ratings”, “Reviews (124)”.
Description: “A very traditional Spanish paella, garnished with Spanish chorizo, chicken, squid, mussels, shrimp, and peas.”
Time: “Prep Time: 45 mins”; “Cook Time: 45 mins”; “Total Time: 1 hr 30 mins”; “Servings: 6”.
Ingredients (1X): 4 tablespoons olive oil; 1 onion, chopped; 2 cloves garlic, minced; 1 red bell pepper, chopped; 4 ounces Spanish chorizo, casing discarded and cut into 1/4-inch dice; 2 skinless, boneless chicken breast halves, cut into 1-inch cubes; 1 (12 ounce) package uncooked Arborio rice; 5 cups chicken broth; ½ cup white wine; 1 sprig fresh thyme; 1 pinch saffron; salt to taste; ground black pepper to taste; 2 squid, cleaned and cut into 1-inch pieces; 2 tomatoes, seeded and chopped; ½ cup frozen green peas; 12 large shrimp, peeled and deveined; 1 pound mussels, cleaned and debearded; ¼ cup chopped Italian flat leaf parsley; 8 slices lemon for garnish.
Directions: Sauté onion, garlic, pepper in olive oil. Add chorizo, chicken and rice, cook 2–3 minutes. Add 3½ cups broth, wine, thyme, saffron, salt and pepper. Bring to boil then simmer 15 minutes, stirring occasionally. Continue until rice is cooked, adding broth as needed. Add squid, tomatoes and peas for 2 minutes, arrange prawns and mussels on top, cover with foil 3–5 minutes. Finish with parsley and lemon.
