Name: Slow Cooker Beef Stew
Rating: 4.5 out of 5; 5,504 ratings; 4,045 Reviews.

Cook Time: 4 hrs. Prep Time: 20 mins. Total Time: 4 hrs 20 mins.
Directions: Place beef in the slow cooker. Cover, and cook until beef is tender enough to cut with a spoon, on Low for 8 to 12 hours, or on High for 4 to 6 hours.
Ingredients in listed order, original recipe 1X (6 servings):
1. 2 pounds beef stew meat, cut into 1-inch pieces
2. ¼ cup all-purpose flour
3. ½ teaspoon salt
4. ½ teaspoon ground black pepper
5. 1 ½ cups beef broth
