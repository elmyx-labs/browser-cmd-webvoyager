Storage: These crab, avocado and cucumber rolls are best eaten the day they are made. To keep leftovers overnight, wrap tightly in plastic wrap, put in an airtight container and refrigerate promptly. Do not leave them at room temperature longer than 1–2 hours. Refrigeration makes the rice firmer and the nori softer. This guidance comes from [Oh My Food Recipes’ California roll storage advice](https://www.ohmyfoodrecipes.com/homemade-california-roll/).
