Ingredients (original 1X batch)
- 1 cup softened butter
- 1 cup white sugar
- 1 cup packed brown sugar
- 2 large eggs, at room temperature
- 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
- 1 teaspoon baking soda
- 2 teaspoons hot water
- ½ teaspoon salt
- 3 cups all-purpose flour
- 2 cups semisweet chocolate chips
- 1 cup chopped walnuts (optional)

Preparation
1. Heat the oven to 350°F (175°C). Use softened butter and room-temperature eggs.
2. With an electric mixer, cream the butter and both sugars until light and creamy, about 4 minutes.
3. Beat in the eggs individually, then mix in the vanilla.
4. Dissolve the baking soda in the hot water. Mix this into the batter with the salt.
5. Add flour, stirring only until the dry streaks disappear. Fold in the chocolate chips and optional walnuts.
6. Place rounded 1½-tablespoon scoops of dough on ungreased baking sheets, leaving 2 inches between them.
7. Bake for about 10 minutes, until the edges are lightly browned and the centers have just set.
8. Cool briefly on the baking sheets, then move to a wire rack to finish cooling. Serve or store in an airtight container.
