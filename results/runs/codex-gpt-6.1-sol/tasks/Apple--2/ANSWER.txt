FINAL: The iPhone 14 Pro and iPhone 15 Pro both launched at a U.S. starting price of $999 before trade-in—a $0 difference. The iPhone 14 Pro uses A16 Bionic; the iPhone 15 Pro uses A17 Pro.

Model           U.S. starting launch price    Chip
 iPhone 14 Pro   $999                          A16 Bionic
 iPhone 15 Pro   $999                          A17 Pro

Apple's current comparison lists both as “Available at authorized resellers” and does not provide current Apple selling prices. The $999 figures are original launch prices, not current purchase quotes.
