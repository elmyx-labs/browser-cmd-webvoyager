FINAL: Apple’s latest iPad Pro models are the 11-inch and 13-inch iPad Pro with M5. Both offer 256GB, 512GB, 1TB, and 2TB storage.

Apple U.S. purchase prices in USD, with standard glass:

Storage | 11-inch Wi-Fi | 11-inch Wi-Fi + Cellular | 13-inch Wi-Fi | 13-inch Wi-Fi + Cellular
256GB   | $1,199         | $1,399                   | $1,499         | $1,699
512GB   | $1,399         | $1,599                   | $1,699         | $1,899
1TB     | $1,799         | $1,999                   | $2,099         | $2,299
2TB     | $2,299         | $2,499                   | $2,599         | $2,799

Nano-texture glass costs $100 extra and is available only with 1TB or 2TB storage on either size. Prices exclude optional accessories, AppleCare, and trade-in credits.
