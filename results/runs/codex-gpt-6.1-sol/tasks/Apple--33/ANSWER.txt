FINAL: The newest iMac (24-inch, M6) is available in seven colors: Blue, Purple, Pink, Orange, Yellow, Green, and Silver.

Evidence: Apple’s current iMac page identifies the new model as “iMac M6 chip” (https://www.apple.com/imac/). Its Technical Specifications page lists all seven colors under “Colors” (https://www.apple.com/imac/specs/), checked October 27, 2026. Screenshot: final.png.
