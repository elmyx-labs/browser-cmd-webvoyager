FINAL: Apple TV 4K (4th generation, as listed by Apple on October 27, 2026) measures 1.22 inches high × 3.67 inches wide × 3.67 inches deep (31.00 × 93.24 × 93.24 mm) and weighs 7.6 ounces (217 g).

Siri Remote (4th generation) features:
- Find My with Precision Finding, plus the ability to play a sound through Find My or by saying “Siri, find the remote.” Precision Finding requires a compatible iPhone running the latest iOS; availability varies by region.
- Touch-enabled clickpad, Siri microphone and dedicated Siri button.
- Power, Back, TV/Home, Play/Pause, Mute and Volume controls.
- Bluetooth 6 and an IR transmitter; TV or receiver control via IR or CEC.
- Rechargeable battery and USB-C charging through a computer or power adapter; cable and adapter sold separately.
