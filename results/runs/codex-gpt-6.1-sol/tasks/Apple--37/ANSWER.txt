FINAL: iPhone 13 Pro has 5 color options; iPhone 14 Pro and iPhone 15 Pro each have 4.

| Model | Color options |
| --- | --- |
| iPhone 13 Pro | Alpine Green, Silver, Gold, Graphite, Sierra Blue |
| iPhone 14 Pro | Deep Purple, Gold, Silver, Space Black |
| iPhone 15 Pro | Natural Titanium, Blue Titanium, White Titanium, Black Titanium |

The 13 Pro and 14 Pro share Silver and Gold. The 15 Pro uses Titanium finish names throughout, including Blue and Black options.

Evidence: Apple's Compare iPhone models page, with iPhone 13 Pro, iPhone 14 Pro and iPhone 15 Pro selected, accessed October 27, 2026:
