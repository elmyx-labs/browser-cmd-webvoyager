FINAL: Apple provides several ways to reset a forgotten Apple ID password (now called an Apple Account password).

1. Use a trusted device where you are already signed in. On iPhone or iPad, open Settings > [your name] > Sign-In & Security > Change Password and follow the instructions. On Mac, choose Apple menu > System Settings > [your name] > Sign-In & Security > Change Password, enter your Mac unlock password, and follow the instructions.
2. Without a trusted device, choose “Forgot Password?” when signing in and follow the prompts. Apple may verify your identity using your trusted phone number or other recovery factors.
3. Use https://iforgot.apple.com in Safari, or use the Apple Support app on a borrowed device or a device at an Apple Store: Support Tools > Reset Password > Help Someone Else. Apple says the information you enter is not stored on the borrowed device.
4. If you cannot reset the password using a trusted device or recovery factors, request account recovery. Apple says this can take several days or more.

Evidence: Apple Support, “If you forgot your password and can’t sign in to your Apple Account,” published October 5, 2026: https://support.apple.com/en-us/102656
Device instructions: Apple Support, “Change your Apple Account password,” published September 29, 2026: https://support.apple.com/en-us/101567
