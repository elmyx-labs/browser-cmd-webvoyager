Title: If you forgot your password and can’t sign in to your Apple Account
Published October 05, 2026. “Apple ID is now Apple Account.” “The easiest way to reset your password is with a trusted device and your trusted phone number.”
Choose “Forgot Password?” and follow the instructions. Without a trusted device, reset options depend on recovery factors, including a trusted phone number, recovery-contact code, account email, or optional recovery/security keys.
Account recovery: several days or more. Start at iforgot.apple.com using Safari, or the Support app on a borrowed device/Apple Store device: Support Tools > Reset Password > Help Someone Else. Apple says information entered is not stored on the borrowed device.

Title: Change your Apple Account password
Published September 29, 2026.
iPhone/iPad/Apple Watch/Apple Vision Pro: Settings > [your name] > Sign-In & Security > Change Password; follow instructions.
Mac: Apple menu > System Settings > [your name] > Sign-In & Security > Change Password; enter the Mac unlock password and follow instructions.
Apple says a one-hour security delay might be required.
