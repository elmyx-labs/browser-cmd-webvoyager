Source: https://info.arxiv.org/help/withdraw.html
Page: Withdrawing an article
Section: Submission not yet announced
Observed 2026-10-27.
Exact guidance: "If your submission has not yet become publicly available you may delete or delay it."
The user page offers Delete or Unsubmit. Deleting removes the submission from the system; unsubmitting returns it to incomplete status for modification and later resubmission.
If either action is difficult, contact arXiv administrators immediately and include the submission identifier, explanation of the problem, and reason for withdrawal.
