FINAL: arXiv’s current guidance does not state a requirement for a multilingual abstract or specify a separator between abstracts. It requires an English version of the entire paper, placed first, followed by the non-English version; an English abstract alone is insufficient. This policy took effect on February 11, 2026.

Sources: arXiv’s “Non-English submissions” page (https://info.arxiv.org/help/faq/multilang.html), its “Abstract: required” metadata instructions (https://info.arxiv.org/help/prep.html), and its policy update (https://blog.arxiv.org/2026/01/13/non-english-paper-submission-guidelines/).
