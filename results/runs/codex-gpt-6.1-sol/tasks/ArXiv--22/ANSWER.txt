FINAL: In arXiv Help, choose “Subscribe to E-Mail Listings” under User Accounts. The “Subscribe to daily listing emails” page explains how to receive daily abstracts of new submissions for a category.

Send a plain ASCII text email to the relevant archive address, with the subject “subscribe Your Full Name” and a body containing “add” followed by the category name or short subject-class code. For example, to subscribe to Risk Management (q-fin.RM):

To: q-fin@arxiv.org
Subject: subscribe Your Full Name

add RM

Replace “Your Full Name” with your name. Rich-text emails are ignored, as are UTF-8 characters in the subject. For a subset of astro-ph, cond-mat, or nlin categories, send the subscription through physics@arxiv.org and specify the desired category names in “add” lines.

Source: https://info.arxiv.org/help/subscribe.html
