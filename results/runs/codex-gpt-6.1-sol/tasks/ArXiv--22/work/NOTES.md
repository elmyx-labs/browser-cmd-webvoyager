Heading: Subscribe to daily listing emails.
The service sends daily listings of abstracts of new submissions. Email must be plain ASCII text; rich text is ignored, as are UTF-8 characters in the Subject field.
Choose the archive address from the list of available archives. For archives with subject classes, specify categories in the message body.
Exact example: To: q-fin@arxiv.org; Subject: subscribe John Smith; body: add RM. This subscribes to Risk Management (q-fin.RM).
The physics archive is the only way to select a subset of subject classes in astro-ph, cond-mat, and nlin. Example: To physics@arxiv.org; Subject subscribe John Smith; body add Soft Condensed Matter and add Superconductivity.
