Submission date: 26 October 2026, at 09:58:46 UTC (version 1). It is the first primary-category entry in ArXiv’s Chaotic Dynamics listing dated 27 October 2026.

Abstract summary: The paper reviews entropy and related measures for sequential data, connecting Shannon entropy, algorithmic complexity and ergodic theory. It explains how Lempel–Ziv factorisation estimates entropy density and presents finite-sequence methods for complexity and transfer entropy. An open-source parallel implementation is illustrated through applications to brain connectivity, coupled nonlinear oscillators and atrial-fibrillation classification from ECG data.
