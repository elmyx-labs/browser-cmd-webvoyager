• Women should follow their personalised birth plans for delivery at Royal Devon & Exeter or their next nearest hospital. For mothers in Barnstaple, the Exeter journey is 53 miles (85km).
• The trust is recruiting temporary and permanent staff, requesting wider NHS support and seeking staff internationally to enable reopening.
