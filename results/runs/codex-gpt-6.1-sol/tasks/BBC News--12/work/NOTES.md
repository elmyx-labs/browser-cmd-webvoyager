BBC Travel, “How US tech helped create modern Italian gelato”, Amy Bizzarri, 14 August 2026.
Picture description: “Two ands hold up cones of colourful gelato on the on the streets of Florence, Italy (Credit: Getty Images)”.
Origin evidence: “Gelato is synonymous with Italy, but its modern origins go back to a humble dairy shop in Tuscany and a couple of abandoned American World War Two freezers.”
The article also traces the template for modern gelato to Bernardo Buontalenti's 16th-century breakthrough in Florence.
