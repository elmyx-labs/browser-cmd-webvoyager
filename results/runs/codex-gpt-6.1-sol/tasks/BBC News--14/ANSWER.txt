FINAL: BBC News — “Are tech job cuts a warning for the wider economy?”
Author: Natalie Sherman, Business reporter, New York.
Published: 6 January 2023.
Source: https://www.bbc.com/news/business-64189106

Key points:
- Amazon announced 18,000 job cuts and Salesforce roughly 8,000. The wave of tech layoffs followed rapid pandemic hiring, with inflation, higher borrowing costs and weaker start-up funding adding pressure.
- The cuts signalled a reset in growth, hiring and investment. With many foreign workers affected, they could be a stronger warning for the global economy than for the US. Expected recessions overseas also threatened tech firms' international business.
- The US labour market remained resilient: December brought 223,000 additional jobs and unemployment of 3.5%. The layoffs did not yet establish a broad recession.
- An expert warned that companies copying one another's layoffs could raise unemployment and turn recession fears into a self-fulfilling downturn.
