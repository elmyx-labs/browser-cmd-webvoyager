Title: Are tech job cuts a warning for the wider economy?
Publication: 6 January 2023
Author: Natalie Sherman; Business reporter, New York.
Article text read with browser-cmd text:
- Amazon announced 18,000 job cuts; Salesforce about 8,000 (10%). Meta, Cisco and Stripe also announced cuts.
- Rising prices pressure spending; higher interest rates increase borrowing costs and reduce start-up funding. Pandemic over-hiring is a major cause.
- Joe Brusuelas described cuts as a necessary adjustment, with implications for growth, employment and investment; foreign staff were heavily affected and the signal could be stronger for the global economy than for the US.
- IMF chief warned a third of the world would likely be in recession in 2023, which would also hurt globally active tech firms.
- US jobs remained resilient: 223,000 added in December and unemployment at 3.5%; displaced tech workers often found work quickly.
- Jeffrey Pfeffer warned imitative layoffs could become self-fulfilling, raising unemployment and worsening the economy.
