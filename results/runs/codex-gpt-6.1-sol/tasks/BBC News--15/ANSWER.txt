FINAL: The current Natural Wonders headline is “A mysterious organ in the sperm whale's head gives it the power to sink ships.” It explains how the spermaceti organ cushions powerful head-first blows, allowing a sperm whale to use its head as a battering ram. The story connects this ability to the sinking of the Essex, which inspired Moby Dick.
