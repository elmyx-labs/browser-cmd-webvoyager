FINAL: The latest article linked from BBC’s Green Living section is “The Himalayan village where you can see the cosmos” by Vandana K, published 1 September 2025. The section lists it as “The remote village drawing stargazers from around the world.”

Main points:
• Hanle, in India’s Ladakh region, attracts stargazers with exceptionally dark skies and about 270 clear nights a year.
• The Hanle Dark Sky Reserve, established in 2022, supports local livelihoods through homestays, trained astronomy guides and an annual September Star Party.
• Growing tourism brings income but also overcrowding and light pollution, threatening the darkness visitors come to experience.
• Residents protect the skies by switching off lights, using blackout curtains and warmer bulbs, and cutting power after 23:00. Plans to spread astronomy tourism across Ladakh aim to ease pressure on Hanle.
