Main findings: Kenya has confirmed nine Ebola cases and two deaths. The first 20 contacts have left quarantine, while 98 remain. Recommendations reported by BBC: Remain calm and vigilant, promptly report suspected cases and seek hospital care, wash hands, avoid close contact with sick people, and avoid stigmatising patients or contacts.
