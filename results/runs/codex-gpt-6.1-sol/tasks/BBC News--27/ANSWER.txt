FINAL: The most recent relevant BBC News article is “‘Unique’ Roman artefact on display at museum,” by Alistair Binney and Alex McIntyre, published 27 October 2026 at 06:17 UTC.

Archaeologists unearthed a carved bone box at Milestone Ground in Broadway, Worcestershire. Believed to date to the late Roman period, it was made from roe deer bone, decorated with intricate circular patterns and recovered from a young woman’s grave. Archaeologist Laura Griffin said she had found no comparable example.

Its significance is the potential insight it offers into the lives, beliefs and craftsmanship of people in the north Cotswolds. The box is now part of Broadway Museum and Art Gallery’s Buried Lives exhibition. Other highlights include a stone core potentially up to 40,000 years old, associated with flint-tool production, and a decorated beaker pot possibly dating to 3500–1500 BC. Together, the finds illuminate thousands of years of local human activity.
