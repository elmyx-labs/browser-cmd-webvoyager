Indian workers are paid to record skilled work and household tasks, supplying training data for “physical AI” that controls robots. The recordings capture real-world movements, dexterity and grip that simulations alone cannot provide. Companies select, clean and label the footage for AI developers. The report examines the extra income for workers, consent and privacy, the risk of future job automation, and India's opportunity to move from supplying raw footage to processing higher-value training data.
