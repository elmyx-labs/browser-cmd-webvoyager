FINAL: Storm Boelo brought severe weather to the UK over the weekend of 24–25 October 2026, particularly Scotland and northern England. Flooding and power cuts affected thousands; falling trees killed two men on Sunday, 25 October, in north London and Dumfries and Galloway. Strong winds continued across Scotland and parts of Northern Ireland on Tuesday, 27 October.

Evidence: BBC Weather’s Features section linked to “More high winds hit parts of UK as Storm Boelo clean-up continues,” published 27 October 2026. The article’s caption identifies Scotland and northern England; its opening paragraph says the storm caused flooding and power cuts over the weekend, and the body dates the fatal incidents to Sunday.
