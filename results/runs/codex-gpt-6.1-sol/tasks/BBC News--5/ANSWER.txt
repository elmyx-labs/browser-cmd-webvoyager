FINAL: Human activities causing climate change are primarily the burning of fossil fuels—coal, oil and gas—in homes, factories and transport systems. Burning these fuels releases greenhouse gases, especially carbon dioxide (CO2), which trap heat near Earth's surface and warm the planet.
