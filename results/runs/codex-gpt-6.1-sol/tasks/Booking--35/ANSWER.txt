FINAL: For trip inspiration, I chose Booking.com’s “Your ultimate ten-day Italy itinerary” by Caroline Lupini, published 15 July 2026.

Four places from the article:
• Rome: Explore ancient history at the Colosseum, visit the Vatican, and wander the historic center. The shorter itinerary suggests three nights here.
• Florence: See Renaissance art at the Uffizi and Accademia, then spend an evening in Oltrarno. The article suggests two nights and a roughly 1.5-hour train journey from Rome.
• Cinque Terre: Add two nights for clifftop hiking trails and lunches by the harbors—a coastal break after the city museums.
• Venice: Enjoy the Grand Canal, St Mark’s Basilica, and a vaporetto ride. The article’s shorter route adds one overnight for an introductory visit.
