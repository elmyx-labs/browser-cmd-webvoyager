FINAL: Sustainability is an uncountable noun. Cambridge gives the UK pronunciation as /səˌsteɪ.nəˈbɪl.ə.ti/ and the US pronunciation as /səˌsteɪ.nəˈbɪl.ə.t̬i/.

Definitions:
1. The quality of being able to continue over a period of time.
2. In an environmental context, the quality of causing little or no damage to the environment, allowing something to continue for a long time.
