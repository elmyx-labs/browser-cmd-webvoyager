serendipity; noun [ U ]; formal
UK /ˌser.ənˈdɪp.ə.ti/; US /ˌser.ənˈdɪp.ə.t̬i/
Definition: the fact of finding interesting or valuable things by chance
Example: There is a real element of serendipity in archaeology.
