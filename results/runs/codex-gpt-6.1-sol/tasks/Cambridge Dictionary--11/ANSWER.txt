FINAL: Ameliorate (formal verb) is pronounced /əˈmiːl.jə.reɪt/ in both UK and US English, approximately “uh-MEEL-yuh-rayt.” It means to make a bad or unpleasant situation better. Example: “Foreign aid is badly needed to ameliorate the effects of the drought.”
