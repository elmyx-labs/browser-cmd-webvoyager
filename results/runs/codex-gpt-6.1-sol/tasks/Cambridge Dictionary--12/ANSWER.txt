FINAL: Resilience (noun, uncountable) — UK and US pronunciation: /rɪˈzɪl.jəns/ (rih-ZIL-yuhns). Definition: the ability to recover happiness or success after adversity. Sample sentence: “Trauma researchers emphasize the resilience of the human psyche.”
