FINAL: Concatenate (verb)
Pronunciation: UK /kənˈkæt.ə.neɪt/; US /kənˈkæt̬.ə.neɪt/.
Definition: “to put things together as a connected series”.
Example: “The filename is a series of concatenated words with no spaces.”
Source: Cambridge Dictionary, https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/concatenate
