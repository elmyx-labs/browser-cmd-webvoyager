FINAL: Cryptocurrency is digital money created by a public network instead of a government, using cryptography to secure payments.

Pronunciation (Cambridge IPA):
UK: /ˈkrɪp.təʊˌkʌr.ən.si/
US: /ˈkrɪp.toʊˌkɝː.ən.si/

Two original example sentences:
1. Payment: The online shop accepts cryptocurrency as payment for its products.
2. Investment: She invested a small portion of her savings in cryptocurrency.
