FINAL: Ubiquitous is an adjective meaning “seeming to be everywhere.”

UK pronunciation: /juːˈbɪk.wɪ.təs/
US pronunciation: /juːˈbɪk.wə.t̬əs/

Example sentence (the English entry lists both UK and US pronunciations): “Leather is very much in fashion this season, as is the ubiquitous denim.”

Cambridge’s American Dictionary section defines it as “found or existing everywhere” and additionally lists the US pronunciation /jʊˈbɪk·wɪ·t̬əs, ju-/.

Source: Cambridge Dictionary, https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/ubiquitous (accessed 27 October 2026).
