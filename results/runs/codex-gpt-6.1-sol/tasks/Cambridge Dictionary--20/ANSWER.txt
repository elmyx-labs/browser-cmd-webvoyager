FINAL: altruism (noun, uncountable)
Definition: “willingness to do things that bring advantages to others, even if it results in disadvantage for yourself”.
Pronunciation: UK /ˈæl.tru.ɪ.zəm/; US /ˈæl.tru.ɪ.zəm/.
Example sentence: “She's not known for her altruism.”
Source: Cambridge Dictionary, Cambridge Advanced Learner's Dictionary & Thesaurus, https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/altruism (accessed October 27, 2026).
