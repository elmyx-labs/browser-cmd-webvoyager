FINAL: Reverie (noun, literary) means “(a state of having) pleasant dream-like thoughts.”
UK pronunciation: /ˈrev.ər.i/
US pronunciation: /ˈrev.ɚ.i/
Cambridge example: “He was lost in reverie until he suddenly heard someone behind him.”
Source: Cambridge Dictionary, https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/reverie (accessed 27 October 2026).
