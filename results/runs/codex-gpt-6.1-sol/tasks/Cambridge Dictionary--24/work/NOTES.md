Entry: reverie — noun [C or U], literary.
Definition: (a state of having) pleasant dream-like thoughts.
UK: /ˈrev.ər.i/
US: /ˈrev.ɚ.i/
Example: He was lost in reverie until he suddenly heard someone behind him.
