FINAL: Cambridge Dictionary gives these two meanings of “harmony”:
1. Music: notes accompanying the main tune that add complexity and interest.
2. Agreement: shared ideas, feelings, or actions, or different parts combining pleasingly.

Source: Cambridge Dictionary, “harmony,” American Dictionary section, https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/harmony (accessed October 27, 2026).
