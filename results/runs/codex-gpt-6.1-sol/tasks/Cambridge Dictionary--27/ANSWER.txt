FINAL: Solitude (uncountable noun) means “the situation of being alone without other people.”
Pronunciation: UK /ˈsɒl.ɪ.tʃuːd/; US /ˈsɑː.lə.tuːd/.
Example: “After months of solitude at sea it felt strange to be in company.”
Source: Cambridge Dictionary, https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/solitude (accessed October 27, 2026).
