FINAL: Zeitgeist (noun) means the characteristic ideas, beliefs, and mood of a particular historical era—the spirit of the times.

Pronunciation (both UK and US): /ˈtsaɪt.ɡaɪst/ or /ˈzaɪt.ɡaɪst/, approximately “TSYTE-gyst” or “ZYTE-gyst,” with stress on the first syllable; the vowel in each syllable is the vowel in “my.”

Examples quoted by Cambridge:
• “The public zeitgeist favours non-pharmaceutical treatments.” — Cambridge English Corpus
• “It is something in the water supply or, perhaps, in our culture—our zeitgeist.” — Hansard archive

Source: Cambridge Dictionary, https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/zeitgeist (accessed October 27, 2026).
