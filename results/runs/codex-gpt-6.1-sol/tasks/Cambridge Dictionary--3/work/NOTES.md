Headword: zeitgeist; noun [ S ].
Definition: "the general set of ideas, beliefs, feelings, etc. that is typical of a particular period in history"
UK and US each list /ˈtsaɪt.ɡaɪst/ and /ˈzaɪt.ɡaɪst/.
Examples from Cambridge English Corpus: "The public zeitgeist favours non-pharmaceutical treatments."
Example from Hansard archive: "It is something in the water supply or, perhaps, in our culture—our zeitgeist."
