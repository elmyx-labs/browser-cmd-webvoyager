Title: Could, may and might — English Grammar Today, Cambridge Dictionary.
Read 27 October 2026.
Exact source wording: We use could, may and might to express degrees of possibility. Many native speakers disagree on which one expresses more or less certainty.
Table: The restaurant may close. | It is likely that the restaurant will close.
Table: The restaurant could close. | It is less likely that the restaurant will close.
Table: The restaurant might close. | There is only a possibility that the restaurant will close but no one is very sure.
The page also says that stressing the modal rather than the main verb expresses more uncertainty.
