FINAL: Use fewer with plural countable nouns and less with uncountable nouns.

Cambridge Dictionary’s grammar entry gives these examples:
• Less: “I do less work at weekends than I used to.” Work is uncountable.
• Fewer: “Better cycle routes would mean fewer cars and fewer accidents.” Cars and accidents are countable plurals.

Less is the comparative of little; fewer is the comparative of few. Cambridge notes that less with plural countable nouns is common in informal speech but traditionally considered incorrect.

Before articles, demonstratives, possessives or pronouns, use of: less of with singular nouns and fewer of with plurals. The noun can be omitted when its meaning is clear.
