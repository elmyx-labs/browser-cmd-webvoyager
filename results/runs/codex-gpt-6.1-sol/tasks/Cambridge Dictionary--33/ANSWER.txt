Source: https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/passive-voice

The section “Passive: active and passive” explains that the passive makes the person or thing receiving an action the topic. The person doing the action can be omitted or introduced with “by”. It is useful for changing the focus, or when the doer is unknown, unimportant, or deliberately unnamed.

Cambridge’s examples:
• Without an agent: “These houses were designed in the 1880s.”
• With an agent: “These houses were designed in the 1880s by Edward Barnes.”
