Evidence: Opening table gives statements, questions and commands in direct and indirect forms, including “I’m tired” → “I told them (that) I was tired”; “Are you ready?” → “The nurse asked Joel if/whether he was ready”; “Leave at once!” → “They ordered us to leave at once.”
Guidelines: statements use a reporting clause plus optional that; yes/no questions use if/whether and statement word order; wh-questions retain a wh-word and use statement word order without a question mark; commands use a to-infinitive, with not to for negative commands. Backshift table lists present simple → past simple, present continuous → past continuous, present perfect → past perfect, past simple → past perfect and will → would; past perfect stays unchanged. No backshift needed if still true/relevant or not yet happened. Pronouns depend on speaker and referent. Time/place changes include this→that, now→then, yesterday→the day before, tomorrow→the next/following day, here→there.
