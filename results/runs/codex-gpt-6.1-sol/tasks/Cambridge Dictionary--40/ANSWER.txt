FINAL: Mitigate (verb, formal) means to reduce how harmful, unpleasant, or bad something is.
UK pronunciation: /ˈmɪt.ɪ.ɡeɪt/.
Example: “It is unclear how to mitigate the effects of tourism on the island.”
Source: Cambridge Dictionary, https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/mitigate (accessed 27 October 2026).
