FINAL: Procrastination — UK /prəˌkræs.tɪˈneɪ.ʃən/; US /proʊˌkræs.tɪˈneɪ.ʃən/. Both stress “NAY” (the syllable /neɪ/).
Example: “This is no time for procrastination - we need decisive action.”
Here, procrastination means delaying something that needs to be done.
