FINAL: Gestalt (noun) means a whole whose qualities exceed the sum of its parts.
Pronunciation: UK /ɡəˈʃtælt/; US /ɡəˈʃtɑːlt/.
Cambridge's example: “This new biography is the first to consider fully the writer's gestalt.”
