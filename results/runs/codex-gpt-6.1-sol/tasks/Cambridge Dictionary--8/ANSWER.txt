FINAL: Three different meanings of “dog” in Cambridge Dictionary:
1. Animal (noun): a four-legged animal commonly kept as a pet or used for guarding.
2. Person (noun, slang): a person of a particular type, as in “You lucky dog!”
3. Follow (verb): to follow someone closely and continually.
