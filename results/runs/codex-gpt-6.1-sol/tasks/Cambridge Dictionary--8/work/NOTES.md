ANIMAL, noun [C]: an animal with four legs, commonly kept as a pet, and sometimes used to guard things
PERSON, noun [C], slang: a person of a stated type. Example: You won $1000? You lucky dog!
FOLLOW, verb [T]: to follow someone closely and continually. Example: The scandal seems likely to dog him for months to come.
