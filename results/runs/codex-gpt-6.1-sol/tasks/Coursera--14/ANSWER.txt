FINAL: Foundations of Agile Project Management, offered by Google on Coursera, matches the request. This beginner-level, 3-hour course includes three modules:
1. Introduction to Agile Project Management
2. Adopting an Agile Mindset
3. Explore Popular Agile Frameworks
Module 1 explicitly covers Agile versus Waterfall and the Agile Manifesto’s four values and twelve principles. The course overview also covers Scrum, Kanban, XP, and Lean.
