FINAL: Understanding Einstein: The Special Theory of Relativity, offered by Stanford Online on Coursera, is a beginner course requiring approximately 81 hours.

Coursera marks it “Beginner level” and “No prior experience required.” Its eight displayed module estimates total 81 hours; the suggested schedule is 2 months at 10 hours per week.

Main topics:
- Einstein’s historical context and the origins of special relativity.
- Events, clocks, and reference frames.
- Spacetime and time as the fourth dimension.
- Time dilation and how an object’s measured size changes with velocity.
- The speed-of-light limit, relativity paradoxes, and time travel.
- Travel to the center of the galaxy and back, and evidence confirming the theory.
