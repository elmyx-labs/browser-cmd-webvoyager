FINAL: Coursera for Business supports workforce development with expert-led learning and customizable training; Coursera for Teams packages these benefits in a self-service plan for 2–499 employees.

Coursera for Business advantages:
- Relevant expertise: courses and recognized credentials from leading universities and companies help employees develop business, data, technology and AI skills.
- Tailored training: learning paths, role-focused Skills Tracks and AI-powered tools help align development with business goals. Coursera explicitly describes Skills Tracks as providing role-specific learning paths.
- Enterprise capabilities: the plan for 500+ users adds AI-powered content authoring, single sign-on, APIs and partner integrations. Dedicated customer success support is available, subject to the annual contract threshold for services.
Sources: [Coursera for Business](https://www.coursera.org/business) and [plan comparison](https://www.coursera.org/business/compare-plans).

Coursera for Teams advantages:
- Convenient access: the enterprise learning library includes courses, Professional Certificates, guided projects and short video lessons, as confirmed in the Teams subscription FAQ. Mobile learning is available on iOS and Android.
- Practical administration: self-service tools, dashboards and reports help track engagement and skill development; learning paths can be customized to business goals.
- Recognized achievement: certificates and badges give employees tangible recognition for learning.
- Clear pricing: the pages display $279 per user for 12 months, with a 14-day money-back guarantee.
Sources: [Coursera for Teams, including the subscription FAQ](https://www.coursera.org/business/teams) and [plan comparison](https://www.coursera.org/business/compare-plans).
