FINAL: Programming in C++: A Hands-on Introduction Specialization, offered by Codio on Coursera, is suitable for beginners: Coursera labels it “Beginner level” and says it is intended for people without programming experience.

Learning outcomes:
- Use C++ to automate routine, repetitive tasks.
- Build engaging programs with graphical output.
- Establish computer science fundamentals that transfer to learning other programming languages.
