FINAL: In Google Maps, open Menu → Search settings. This opens Google’s Search Settings page, with two sections:

• Privacy & Safety: Search customisation (On), SafeSearch (Blurring on), and Parental controls on Family Link.
• Other settings: Language and region; Dark theme (Device default); Results in a new window (Off); Spoken answers (On); Auto-complete with trending searches (On).
