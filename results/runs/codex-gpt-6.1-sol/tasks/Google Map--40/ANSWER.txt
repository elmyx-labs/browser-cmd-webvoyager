FINAL: Boston Sail Loft, 80 Atlantic Ave, Boston, MA 02110, meets the requirement: Google Maps shows 4.6/5 from 6,849 reviews. Its official menu lists single and twin steamed New England lobsters and a New England lobster roll.

One-star review: Liz Challoner, displayed as “4 months ago,” complained about seating beside the kitchen and bathrooms, a fryer-grease smell, chowder overwhelmed by dill, overly lemony seafood pasta, and staff cleaning instead of attending to diners. She rated food, service and atmosphere 1/5. I found it by sorting Google reviews by Lowest rating and opened the shared review in Google Maps.
