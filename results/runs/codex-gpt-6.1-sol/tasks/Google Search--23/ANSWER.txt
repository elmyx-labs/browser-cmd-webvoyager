FINAL: LeBron James, born December 30, 1984, in Akron, Ohio, is an American basketball player. A high-school star, he was the Cleveland Cavaliers’ first overall pick in the 2003 NBA draft and earned Rookie of the Year honors in 2004. He won four league MVP awards and NBA championships with Miami (2012, 2013), Cleveland (2016), and the Los Angeles Lakers (2020). In 2023, he became the NBA’s all-time leading scorer.
