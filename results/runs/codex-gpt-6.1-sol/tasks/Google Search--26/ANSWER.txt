FINAL: Adobe Photoshop 27.x is Adobe’s current version family. Its Mac hardware requirements are:

Component | Minimum | Recommended
Processor | Multicore Intel or Apple Silicon | ARM-based Apple Silicon
RAM | 8 GB | 16 GB or more
Video RAM | 1.5 GB | 2 GB
Display | 1280 × 800 | 1920 × 1080 or higher
Storage | 10 GB free for installation, plus extra space during installation | 100 GB free; fast internal SSD for Photoshop; additional high-speed drives or SSDs for scratch disks

Graphics must support Metal. For Intel processors, Adobe lists AVX2 and SSE 4.2 or later. Photoshop cannot be installed on a case-sensitive file system.

Also required: macOS 14.x, 15.x, or 26; Adobe recommends Sequoia 15.6.1. Internet access and registration are required for activation, membership validation, and online services.
