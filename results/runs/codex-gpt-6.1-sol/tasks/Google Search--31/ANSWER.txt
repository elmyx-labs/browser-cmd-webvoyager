FINAL: 2015 — the 2014–15 season. Cristiano Ronaldo scored a career-best 61 goals in all competitions for Real Madrid.

Evidence: UEFA explicitly lists “61: most goals registered in all competitions for Real Madrid in one season (2014/15).” ESPN’s report dated May 23, 2015 confirms that 61 was his personal single-season record; its title, date and record caption appear together in final.png.
