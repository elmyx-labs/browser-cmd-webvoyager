FINAL: Francis Halzen won the most recent Nobel Prize in Physics (2026), announced on 6 October 2026, for his decisive contributions to the IceCube Neutrino Observatory and the discovery of high-energy neutrinos of astrophysical origin. His vision and scientific leadership helped turn a cubic kilometre of South Pole ice into a neutrino detector, enabling a new form of astronomy that studies powerful processes in the distant universe.
