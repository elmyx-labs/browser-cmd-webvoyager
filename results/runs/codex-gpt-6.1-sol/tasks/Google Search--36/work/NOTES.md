Recipient: Francis Halzen, University of Wisconsin–Madison, USA.
Exact award citation: “for decisive contributions to the IceCube Neutrino Observatory and the discovery of high-energy neutrinos of astrophysical origin”.
Contribution: Halzen envisioned and led the development of an observatory using a cubic kilometre of South Pole ice equipped with light sensors. It detects rare neutrino interactions, letting researchers investigate powerful cosmic particle accelerators and the distant universe.
