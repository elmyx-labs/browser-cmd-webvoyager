FINAL: iPhones require iOS 17.1 or later for AirDrop transfers to continue over the internet after moving out of AirDrop range.

Evidence: Apple Support, “About iOS 17 Updates,” iOS 17.1 section, states that this update introduces internet continuation when you step out of AirDrop range.
Source: https://support.apple.com/en-us/118723
