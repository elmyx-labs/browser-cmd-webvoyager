The iOS 17.1 section states: “Content continues to transfer over the internet when you step out of AirDrop range”.
The section introduction identifies iOS 17.1 as the update that introduces this ability for iPhone.
Apple's iOS 17 iPhone User Guide also confirms that a transfer already initiated continues over the internet after leaving Bluetooth or Wi-Fi range:
https://support.apple.com/en-am/guide/iphone/iphcd8b9f0af/17.0/ios/17.0
