FINAL: Three recent, innovative open ASR models on Hugging Face are Cactus Whistle, IBM Granite Speech 5.0 2B, and Fermion Research Phonon-2.

Window checked: September 27–October 27, 2026. “Widely recognized” is assessed through Hugging Face’s Trending ASR listing and public likes/downloads, rather than publisher reputation alone. All three appear on the first page of that listing.

1. Cactus-Compute/whistle — released October 2, 2026; Apache 2.0. Its 16.9 MB model transcribes seven languages on CPU, with live streaming, word timestamps and speech embeddings. Hugging Face shows 524 likes and 61,357 downloads.
   Model: https://huggingface.co/Cactus-Compute/whistle
   Dated release: https://www.cactuscompute.com/blog/whistle
   Hugging Face metadata (repository created September 30): https://huggingface.co/api/models/Cactus-Compute/whistle

2. ibm-granite/granite-speech-5.0-2b — released October 20, 2026, as explicitly stated on its model card; Apache 2.0. It combines multilingual ASR and speech translation, supports keyword lists of up to 2,000 terms, and improves transcription robustness to noise and distant microphones. Hugging Face shows 53 likes and 3,517 downloads.
   Model and release date: https://huggingface.co/ibm-granite/granite-speech-5.0-2b
   Hugging Face metadata (repository created October 7): https://huggingface.co/api/models/ibm-granite/granite-speech-5.0-2b

3. FermionResearch/Phonon-2 — released September 29, 2026; CC-BY-4.0 weights. It compresses NVIDIA Parakeet into a 164 MB download for local English transcription. Its publisher reports 5.21% average word error rate across seven Open ASR test sets and 174× real-time transcription on an M5 MacBook Air. Hugging Face shows 371 likes and 12,264 downloads.
   Model: https://huggingface.co/FermionResearch/Phonon-2
   Dated release: https://www.fermionresearch.com/changelog/ (September 29, “Introducing Phonon-2”)
   Hugging Face metadata (repository created September 28): https://huggingface.co/api/models/FermionResearch/Phonon-2
