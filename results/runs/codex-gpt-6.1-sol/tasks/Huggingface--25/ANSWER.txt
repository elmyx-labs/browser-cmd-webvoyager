FINAL: In Hugging Face’s “Load adapters with 🤗 PEFT” tutorial (Transformers v4.49.0), see “Load in 8bit or 4bit.” Use bitsandbytes quantization to save memory:

```python
from transformers import AutoModelForCausalLM, BitsAndBytesConfig

model = AutoModelForCausalLM.from_pretrained(
    "ybelkada/opt-350m-lora",
    quantization_config=BitsAndBytesConfig(load_in_8bit=True),
    device_map="auto",
)
```

For 4-bit loading, replace load_in_8bit=True with load_in_4bit=True. device_map="auto" distributes the model across available hardware.

Evidence: The tutorial’s “Load in 8bit or 4bit” section states both options and recommends device_map="auto"; its example passes BitsAndBytesConfig(load_in_8bit=True) through quantization_config.
