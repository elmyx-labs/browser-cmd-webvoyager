FINAL: Hugging Face offers three paid subscription plans—PRO, Team and Enterprise—plus free entry-level hardware and usage-based storage and compute services. Prices below were displayed on October 27, 2026.

| Plan | Advertised price | Advantages |
|---|---|---|
| PRO Account | $9/month | For individuals: 10× private storage capacity, 2× public storage capacity, $2 in monthly inference credits, and 8× ZeroGPU quota with highest queue priority. Also includes hosting ZeroGPU, Gradio and Docker Spaces; Spaces Dev Mode with SSH/VS Code; personal blog publishing; Dataset Viewer for private datasets; early feature access; and a PRO badge. |
| Team | $20 per user/month | For growing organizations: SSO via SAML/OIDC; data-location control through Storage Regions; Audit Logs; granular permissions through Resource Groups; repository analytics; advanced authentication and repository-visibility policies; centralized token control and approvals; private Dataset Viewer; and Gradio/Docker Spaces with advanced compute options. All organization members receive the ZeroGPU and Inference Providers PRO benefits. Available through self-service credit-card signup. |
| Enterprise | $50 per user/month | All Team benefits, plus the highest storage, bandwidth and API rate limits; automated user management through SCIM provisioning; advanced security and access controls; custom onboarding; managed billing with annual commitments; legal/compliance processes; and dedicated support. Contact sales to arrange it. |

Additional pricing options

• Storage: Public/private repositories cost $12/$18 per TB/month at the base tier, $10/$16 at 50TB+, $9/$14 at 200TB+, and $8/$12 at 500TB+. Advantages: volume discounts, with egress and CDN included at no extra cost. Above 500TB, custom pricing is available through sales.
• Spaces hardware: CPU Basic (2 vCPU, 16GB RAM) and ZeroGPU are listed as free. CPU Upgrade costs $0.03/hour; paid GPU hardware starts at $0.40/hour for a small Nvidia T4. Advantage: on-demand hardware for sharing ML applications and demos, with larger and custom configurations available.
• Inference Endpoints: Advertised from $0.033/hour, with CPU, GPU and accelerator configurations. Advantages: dedicated, autoscaling infrastructure for secure, production-ready model deployment with no cold starts.
