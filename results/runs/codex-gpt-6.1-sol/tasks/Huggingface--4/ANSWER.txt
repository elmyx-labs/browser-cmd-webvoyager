FINAL: Zephyr-7B-β (HuggingFaceH4/zephyr-7b-beta) is an open-source conversational AI model on Hugging Face, trained primarily in English and released under the MIT license.

Main features:
- 7-billion-parameter GPT-like model fine-tuned from Mistral-7B-v0.1.
- Trained as a helpful assistant using synthetic dialogue data and Direct Preference Optimization (DPO), including UltraChat and UltraFeedback.
- Supports conversational text generation through Hugging Face Transformers, with chat templates and system/user messages.

Applications:
- English-language chatbots and interactive conversational assistants.
- Chatbots with a specified response style; the model card demonstrates a pirate-style assistant.
- Research and experimentation with dialogue fine-tuning and preference alignment; the card links the training-code repository and datasets.
