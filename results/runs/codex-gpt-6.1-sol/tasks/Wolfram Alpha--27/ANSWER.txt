FINAL: At 25 °C, Copper (Cu) has thermal conductivity 398 W/(m·K), and Aluminum (Al) has thermal conductivity 237 W/(m·K), according to BYU's Thermal Properties of Pure Metals table.

Evidence: https://cleanroom.byu.edu/thermal_properties — the table explicitly says “At 25°C” and lists Copper as 3.98 and Aluminum as 2.37 watts/cm°C, equivalent to 398 and 237 W/(m·K). Screenshot: final.png.
