FINAL: The series Σ(n=1 to ∞) 1/(n³ + 1) converges (absolutely).

For every n ≥ 1, 0 < 1/(n³ + 1) ≤ 1/n³. Since Σ 1/n³ is a convergent p-series with p = 3 > 1, the comparison test proves convergence.
