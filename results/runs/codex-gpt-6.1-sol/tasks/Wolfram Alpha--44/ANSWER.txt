FINAL: g(x) = 2 arctan(tanh(artanh(tan(1/2)) − x/2)), valid for all real x, with angles in radians.

In the general solution g(x) = 2 arctan(tanh((C − x)/2)), the condition g(0) = 1 gives tanh(C/2) = tan(1/2). Therefore C = 2 artanh(tan(1/2)).
